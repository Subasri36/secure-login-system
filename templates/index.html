What's under the hood

Hashed, never stored plain

Passwords are salted and hashed with bcrypt (12 rounds) before they ever touch the database.

Validated at the door

Usernames, emails, and password strength are checked server-side, and every query is parameterized against SQL injection.

Sessions that expire

Signed, HTTP-only session cookies with a 30-minute lifetime, plus a clean logout that clears everything.

Optional two-factor

Turn on TOTP-based 2FA from your dashboard and pair it with any authenticator app in under a minute.
